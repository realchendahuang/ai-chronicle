In November 2023, 01.AI released the Yi model series. The company carried a halo from the day it was announced—founder Kai-Fu Lee, former president of Google China and founder of Sinovation Ventures, is among the most recognizable names in Chinese tech. When such a figure enters the model race, expectations run higher than for ordinary startups from the start. Yi-34B did not disappoint: with a long context window and solid bilingual ability, it reached the top of open-model leaderboards.

Yi's success owed much to 01.AI's insistence on self-research. The market was full of startups that "wrapped" or finetuned existing open models; 01.AI chose to build its own stack from pretraining, keeping data, compute, and algorithms in its own hands. Yi-34B's balanced English and Chinese results earned recognition in the overseas open-source community, making it a representative of Chinese models going global.

01.AI's story is also a footnote to the "six little dragons" landscape. Alongside Moonshot, Zhipu, MiniMax, and StepFun, 01.AI rode the golden window of Chinese model entrepreneurship, using a star founder to leverage capital and talent, then proving itself through products and models. After Yi, the company released versions like Yi-Lightning and gradually shifted toward enterprise services.

Of course, a star founder's company carries star-level pressure. Expectations from capital were high and competition fast; 01.AI also went through exploration and adjustment on the path to commercialization. But the technical achievement of Yi-34B is real—it proved that even under compute constraints in a startup environment, Chinese teams can train open models that enter the global first tier.

Looking back at 01.AI's start, its most valuable legacy is not a parameter number but a demonstration effect: when a top investor is willing to take on the heaviest self-research work, it says the industry's imagination is large enough. With the Yi series, Lee completed an identity shift from "investor" to "founder"—and Yi-34B is the most direct evidence of that transformation.
