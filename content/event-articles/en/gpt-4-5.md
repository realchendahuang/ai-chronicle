On February 27, 2025 OpenAI released GPT-4.5. By parameters and benchmarks alone it was a solid flagship upgrade; but the positioning OpenAI gave it lent the release a different flavor—they said this would be the last flagship "not based on reasoning." That sentence was an official announcement: an era was ending.

GPT-4.5 strengthened the less "hardcore" but experience-critical capabilities: more natural writing, higher-EQ understanding, stronger pattern recognition, and fewer hallucinations. It did not emphasize the extreme math-and-code reasoning of the o-series; it did "expressing like a human" better than anything at the time. For content creation, everyday-assistant, and creative-scenario users, GPT-4.5's experience improvement was immediate.

The route dispute behind the release was more notable than the product. In 2024 o1's success sparked internal debate: keep burning money on pretraining scale, or shift to test-time compute? GPT-4.5 was widely seen as the high point and summary of the "pure-scaling route"—it proved this path still yields excellent products, while frankly acknowledging diminishing returns. The polarized market reaction mirrored that dispute: some praised the experience, others questioned the lack of reasoning breakthroughs.

GPT-4.5's release actually set the stage for the next era. OpenAI made clear that the future GPT-5 would unify reasoning and fast answers, with no more standalone "pure model" flagships. That declaration turned the industry's gaze toward new directions: test-time compute, reasoning capability, unified architecture. Later events confirmed it—GPT-5 walked that road, and GPT-4.5, as the farewell performance of the old route, made a dignified exit.

The industry impact was lasting. GPT-4.5 made "the pure-scaling era is ending" a consensus, and vendors shifted R&D weight from "bigger pretraining" to "more test-time compute." From late 2025 through 2026, reasoning models, thinking modes, and long-task execution became flagship standards—none originated with GPT-4.5, but its posture of release sent the formal signal for the turn.

Looking back at February 2025, GPT-4.5's historical value lies precisely in its "endingness." With a mature, usable, excellent product, it drew the line under the brute-force-scaling route and announced the full arrival of the reasoning era. An era often ends not with a bang but like this—handing in a final polished paper, then turning to let the next generation take the stage.
