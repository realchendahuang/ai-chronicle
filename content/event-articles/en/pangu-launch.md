In April 2021, at Huawei Cloud's Developer Conference, Huawei released the Pangu foundation-model family. Unlike the general chatbots the world knew at the time, Pangu was built for industry from day one: NLP, vision, multimodal, and scientific-computing variants. Its goal was not to chat with you but to work in mines, weather services, pharma, and manufacturing. In the GPT-3-fueled year of 2021, this counted as a deliberately contrarian route.

Huawei's confidence had two foundations: the Ascend-chip-driven domestic compute ecosystem on one end, and Huawei's deep, long-standing industry relationships on the other. Telecom, energy, manufacturing, and government customers rarely wanted "a bit of everything and nothing well"—they wanted "a model that understands my industry." Pangu's "industry foundation model" positioning aimed precisely at that need.

Pangu earned attention through real deployments. Its weather model predicted conditions with accuracy that at times beat traditional numerical forecasting; in coal mines it helped spot unsafe operations; in pharma it assisted with protein and drug-discovery research. These were not keynote animations but applications in real production environments—foundation models first appearing as "industrial tools" rather than "chat toys."

Pangu's route also reflects the diversity of Chinese foundation models. While some companies focused on general chat and others chased parameter counts, Huawei chose a heavier road: go deep, bind to industries, and win trust through scenarios. This "vertical depth" route was later borrowed by many domestic vendors and became a key part of "enterprise AI."

Looking back at Pangu's start, it reminds us that the history of foundation models is not written only by chat windows and leaderboards. The 2021 choice to send AI into mines and factories produced an early answer to "can foundation models create value in real industry?" And by 2026, when vertical models became mainstream narrative, Pangu was among the first teams to plant its flag inside the industry itself.
