In April 2023 Alibaba Cloud announced that Tongyi Qianwen was opening for testing—the first time Alibaba's self-developed large language model appeared publicly. Amid the post-ChatGPT wave, Chinese big companies were rolling out their own models, and Qianwen stood out: backed by Alibaba Cloud, it had ready enterprise customers and compute infrastructure, positioned from the start not just as a "chatbot" but as "the foundation model on the cloud."

Qianwen's trajectory took a decisive turn in summer 2023. In August, Alibaba open-sourced Qwen-7B and Qwen-14B. The decision looked ordinary then but proved momentous: with strong Chinese ability, good multilingual performance, and a permissive license, Qwen was rapidly adopted by developers worldwide, becoming one of the most popular Chinese open models overseas. The Chinese product name "Tongyi Qianwen" gave way to "Qwen" as an international model brand.

Walking on two legs—open and closed—was the core of Alibaba's model strategy. Inwardly, Qianwen served enterprise customers through Alibaba Cloud API and integrated with DingTalk and Taobao; outwardly, the open-source Qwen line kept iterating, from 7B to 72B to MoE architectures, always near the frontier of open models. This route let Alibaba own the "model plus cloud" bundling play: build the ecosystem with open source, monetize through cloud services.

Qianwen's significance lies in demonstrating the standard posture for a cloud vendor in the model race. Not fighting alone with apps, but exporting the model as cloud infrastructure, offsetting the gap of any single technical advantage with the scale of the ecosystem. By 2026, with Qwen3.8 a major force in global open and accessible models, this route has proven long and stable.

Looking back at that low-key testing announcement in April 2023, it opened a long road to internationalization. From a Chinese chat app to an open-model family used by developers worldwide, Qianwen completed its identity transformation in three years. And the starting point was simply "opening for testing" one spring day.
