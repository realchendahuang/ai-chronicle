In August 2023 MiniMax released its self-developed large models and launched the Hailuo AI chatbot at the same time. The company, founded by Yan Junjie, had existed since 2021—before the ChatGPT wave. MiniMax's self-positioning was clear from the start: multimodal and efficiency in equal measure. In an era when domestic models commonly chased parameters and scale, this company behaved like a technical school—it aimed not at merely bigger models but at smarter architectures.

MiniMax truly turned heads with the MiniMax-01 line in 2025. That generation used a new MoE architecture, paired with a context window in the millions of tokens, and introduced linear-attention ideas to cut the cost of long-sequence inference. The combination of long context and efficient architecture caused a stir in the overseas developer community—it wasn't parameter stacking but architecture design that delivered long-text capability and cost control at once.

On the product side, MiniMax insisted on walking on two legs: opening APIs to developers while operating consumer products like Hailuo AI for chat and video generation. Multimodal ability extended from text to video generation, giving Hailuo a place among domestic AI apps. A technical-school temperament plus productization gave MiniMax a third road distinct from "pure chat" and "pure open source."

MiniMax's differentiation is highly representative among domestic models. When many assumed "Chinese companies can only catch up overseas," MiniMax-01 proved Chinese teams can innovate at the architecture level and even lead on certain metrics. This was not just a company's victory but a reset of outsiders' judgment about domestic technical capability.

Looking back at MiniMax's journey, the most striking quality is a clear-headed refusal to fight on the same track as everyone else. While the market compared parameter sizes and leaderboard scores, it chose to go deep on long context and architectural efficiency. By 2026, the domestic model landscape is no longer merely a parameter race—and MiniMax is one of the earliest companies to open a new dimension in that race with the weapon of "new architecture."
