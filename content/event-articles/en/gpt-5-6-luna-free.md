On August 7, 2026, OpenAI announced something that at any other company would be a changelog entry: free and Go users' default model switched to GPT-5.6 Luna, with unlimited text chat starting next week. Files, images, and voice remain capped—but text stops counting. For ordinary users who open ChatGPT a few times a day, this is the first time the free tier has run the current flagship.

The more notable piece is a button called Think. Deep reasoning used to live behind the paywall: free users got fast answers, and "think harder" required a subscription. The Think button gives the free tier its first on-demand higher reasoning—not occasionally granted by the system, but pressed by the user. Reasoning moved from subscription privilege to product parameter, and that shift says more about OpenAI's repositioning of the free tier than any leaderboard: the free door is no longer a window into old models but a distribution channel for the current flagship.

The same day, GPT-5.6 Sol for Plus/Pro also upgraded: the company reported roughly 68% fewer factual errors than GPT-5.5 Instant, plus a new thinking-effort slider. The 68% is a vendor claim and should be read with that boundary; but making "factual-error rate" a quantifiable selling point shows OpenAI moving quality competition from "who is smarter" to "who makes fewer mistakes." The effort slider hands the reasoning budget to the user—faster answers or steadier ones, your call.

Stepping back, this update is the third step after the GPT-5.6 family launch on July 9 and the API price cuts on July 31 (Luna -80%, Terra -20%). Model release, price restructuring, free access—three steps in one month. With Luna's API price near zero, unlimited text chat on the free tier became cost-feasible; OpenAI is using Luna's scale economics to make "latest model" the default free experience.

For the industry, the question turns sharp: when the free tier runs the current flagship with on-demand reasoning, what is left inside the paywall? For users, the answer is simpler: the button that used to sit locked behind the subscription wall can now be pressed. The free tier's positioning moved from "try an old model" to "try the current flagship"—and that step may change ordinary habits more than any single model release.
