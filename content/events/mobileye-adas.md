summary: 希伯来大学教授沙舒亚创办 Mobileye，用单目摄像头和专用视觉芯片做量产车的辅助驾驶感知。它把「机器看懂道路」从论文变成前装量产件，成为自动驾驶产业最早也最成功的商业化路线之一。
summaryEn: Hebrew University professor Amnon Shashua founded Mobileye, using a single camera and dedicated vision chip for driver-assistance perception in production cars. It turned "machines reading the road" from papers into factory-installed parts—one of the earliest, most successful commercializations of autonomous driving.
background: 1990 年代末，自动驾驶的算法研究主要停留在实验室，量产车几乎没有视觉感知。沙舒亚的研究方向是视觉几何与三维重建，他相信可以用相对便宜的摄像头+专用芯片实现可靠的车道与障碍物感知。
backgroundEn: In the late 1990s autonomous-driving algorithms stayed mostly in labs and production cars had almost no vision perception. Shashua's research in visual geometry and 3D reconstruction convinced him a relatively cheap camera-plus-chip combo could deliver reliable lane and obstacle perception.
whatHappened: Mobileye 走「前装量产」路线：把视觉感知算法固化进专用芯片（EyeQ 系列），打包给车企作为 ADAS 方案。从早期车道偏离预警到后来的自动紧急制动，Mobileye 芯片被数以千万计的汽车搭载，成为全球 ADAS 的事实标准之一。
whatHappenedEn: "Mobileye took the \"factory-installed production\" route: hardening vision algorithms into dedicated chips (the EyeQ series) and selling automakers an ADAS package. From early lane-departure warnings to later automatic emergency braking, Mobileye chips shipped in tens of millions of cars and became a de facto ADAS standard."
whyImportant: Mobileye 定义了「视觉感知量产化」的商业模式：算法+专用芯片+整车合作。它让计算机视觉大规模进入真实道路，也为后来特斯拉、Waymo、华为等各路自动驾驶路线提供了参照系。
whyImportantEn: "Mobileye defined the \"vision perception at scale\" business: algorithms plus dedicated chips plus automaker partnerships. It brought computer vision onto real roads at scale and became a reference point for every later autonomy route from Tesla and Waymo to Huawei."
