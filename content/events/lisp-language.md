summaryEn: John McCarthy introduced LISP, a language built around symbolic expressions and recursion. It became the dominant tool of AI research for decades and helped establish the idea that programs can be treated as data.
background: 达特茅斯会议之后，研究者需要一种能处理符号而不是只有数字的语言；FORTRAN 并不适合这类任务。
backgroundEn: After the Dartmouth workshop, researchers needed a language for symbols rather than numbers alone; FORTRAN did not fit the job.
whatHappened: 1958 年麦卡锡发表《Programs with Common Sense》，提出建议接受者与咨询者系统。随后他在 MIT 开始设计 LISP，1960 年发表描述其核心的论文《Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine》。语言以列表为基本结构，函数可把程序本身当数据处理。
whatHappenedEn: McCarthy published "Programs with Common Sense" in 1958, proposing an advice-taker system. He then began designing LISP at MIT, describing its core in the 1960 paper "Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine." Lists were the basic structure, and functions could treat programs themselves as data.
whyImportant: LISP 把「符号推理」这一 AI 早期路线变成可写可跑的工程实践。它的词法作用域、垃圾回收与函数式思想影响了许多后来的语言，而「代码即数据」直接通向元编程与自我修改程序的研究。
whyImportantEn: LISP turned the early symbolic-AI program into practical engineering. Its lexical scoping, garbage collection, and functional ideas influenced many later languages, while "code as data" led directly to metaprogramming and self-modifying programs.
impact:
  developer: 为 AI 与程序语言设计提供了一棵影响深远的技术树。

impactEn:
  developer: Provided a far-reaching technical lineage for AI and language design.
  industry: Powered many commercial tools of the expert-systems era.
