backgroundEn: In 2001 DARPA, tasked by Congress, launched an autonomous-vehicle competition to accelerate the technology through open challenge, drawing university teams and startups.

whatHappenedEn: The 2004 race in the Mojave Desert saw no finishers; five cars finished in 2005; the 2007 Urban Challenge moved to a mock city where vehicles had to obey traffic rules over roughly 60 miles. The three races seeded many self-driving startup teams.
