whatHappenedEn: Siri began as a SRI International spin-off acquired by Apple in 2010. At the event Tim Cook and Scott Forstall demonstrated conversational queries about weather, restaurants, and reminders. The system recognized speech, understood requests, and called web services for answers—the start of Apple's voice entrance.
